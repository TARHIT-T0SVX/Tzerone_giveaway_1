HOW TO PLAY

  • Move: Drag the joystick anywhere on the left, or WASD / Arrow Keys
  • Attack: Sword button (or J / tap canvas) — chain 3 hits for a combo finisher
  • Heavy: Hammer button (or K) — slow, huge damage and knockback
  • Dash: Bolt button (or Shift) — dodge through attacks with invincibility
  • Block: Hold the shield (or B) — block right before a hit to PARRY and stun
  • Survive waves, defeat a boss every 5th wave, spend Gold in the Barracks
